HUR Pneumatic Machines

Unlike your standard gym machines, HUR machines use air-powered resistance instead of traditional weight stacks, creating smoother movement and less impact on the joints. The resistance can be adjusted precisely, making it suitable for beginners, older adults, and progressive strength training. Combined with coach supervision, HUR supports safer and more comfortable exercise.

Leg Extension / Leg Curl machine

Helps with improving lower-body strength used in walking, climbing stairs, and standing up from a chair.

Typical gym:

Uses a weight stack and larger weight jumps, which can feel heavy when starting the movement and harder to fine-tune.

HUR at LC:

Uses smooth air-pressure resistance with small, precise adjustments, creating a more controlled knee exercise with less momentum and less strain on the joints.
