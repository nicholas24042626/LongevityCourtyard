-------- WHY ACTIVE AGEING MATTERS

Muscle strength, balance and mobility naturally decline with age, but research consistently shows this decline can be slowed and even reversed with regular, guided strength training.

Better Balance, Fewer Falls

Balance and lower-body exercises help reduce fall risk while building confidence in everyday movement.

Stronger Muscles, Longer Independence

Resistance training preserves muscle, supports bone health, and helps maintain independence as we age.

Mind & Mood Wellbeing

Regular activity supports mood, memory and long-term brain health through increased blood flow.

Longevity Courtyard is not just a gym. A programme built to help you stay independent.

-------- WHO WE ARE

Longevity Courtyard is a preventive active ageing programme in Jurong West, run under the Tzu-Chi Foundation. Sessions take place on HUR strength equipment, designed for older bodies. Every session is guided by a coach — never self-service, never intimidating.

Functional strength training that mirrors real, everyday movement

A preventive healthcare approach, not a fitness fad
