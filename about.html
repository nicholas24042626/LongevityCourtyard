About Shangri-La

About Shangri-La

As we age, strength, balance, and mobility can gradually decline. The Shangri-La Programme was created to provide a safe, structured, and supportive environment where adults can train with confidence through assessment, supervision, and progressive exercise.

Professional Coaching

Exercise confidently with experienced coaches guiding every step of your journey.

Suitable for everyone

Equipment and pacing can be adjusted to your strength and needs.

Safe Environment

Build strength in a welcoming space designed for safe and effective active ageing.

Lasting Independence

Improve your strength, mobility, and confidence to stay active every day.

-------- Designed for smooth, adjustable resistance

The programme uses HUR resistance equipment together with functional movement training, balance work, and mobility exercises. Resistance is adjusted progressively based on the participant’s assessment and ongoing progress.

Why HUR and Structured Progression

Smooth adjustable resistance Supports both foundational and progressive training Combined with functional and balance exercises Progressed according to individual ability

Supervised Group Sessions

“Sessions are conducted in supervised groups of up to 10 participants. A coach demonstrates the exercises, monitors safety, provides individual adjustments when needed, and supports participants throughout the session.”

Holistic Approach

We focus on more than just exercise. By improving strength, balance, mobility, and confidence together, our programmes help older adults move with greater ease and maintain their independence in everyday life.

Personalised Progress

Rather than chasing quick results, we focus on gradual, sustainable progress. You'll develop healthy habits and practical exercises that continue to benefit you long after each session.

Evidence-Based Tracking

Using HUR smart exercise equipment and Tanita body composition analysis, we track your progress over time. This allows your coach to personalise your programme, monitor improvements, and keep you motivated as you grow stronger.

The Longevity Courtyard Difference

Functional Strength

We build strength that supports everyday living. Every exercise is designed to improve the movements you rely on daily—from climbing stairs and carrying groceries to getting out of a chair with confidence.
