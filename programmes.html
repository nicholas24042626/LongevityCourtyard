Longevity Courtyard

As we age, strength, balance, and mobility can gradually decline. Longevity Courtyard was created to provide a safe, structured, and supportive environment where adults can train with confidence through assessment, supervision, and progressive exercise.

Professional Coaching

Exercise confidently with experienced coaches guiding every step of your journey.

Suitable for everyone

Equipment and pacing can be adjusted to your strength and needs.

Safe Environment

Build strength in a welcoming space designed for safe and effective active ageing.

Lasting Independence

Improve your strength, mobility, and confidence to stay active every day.

Types of Longevity Courtyard Sessions

Longevity Courtyard offers both Functional Strength Training and Stretch & Strength sessions. One focuses on building strength, balance, and mobility for everyday activities, while the other supports flexibility, relaxation, and overall wellness.

Functional Strength Training

Build the strength needed for everyday activities such as walking, climbing stairs, carrying groceries, and getting up from a chair. Exercises are designed to improve muscle strength, balance, and confidence in daily life.

Stretch and Strength

Stretch and Strength sessions happen on Tuesdays and Thursdays. They focus on engaging the body through yoga and stretches for relaxation, wellness and flexibility.

Shangri-La

The Shangri-La programme is an extension of Longevity Courtyard. On top of Longevity Courtyard sessions, it includes a Nutrition and a Sleep Therapy session to improve one’s wellness through lifestyle habits.

Nutrition

Nutrition lessons to guide healthy eating habits that support energy, strength, and healthy ageing.

Sleep Therapy

Relaxation and sleep-support techniques to improve rest, recovery, and overall well-being.

Pre/Post Assessment

Pre-Assessment is done at the start of the programme. Post-Assessment happens after 3 months.
