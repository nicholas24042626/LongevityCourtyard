Ice believes movement is about far more than exercise. With a background in contemporary dance, yoga, and community arts, she helps older adults rediscover confidence, improve mobility, and express themselves through purposeful movement. At Tzu Chi, she combines creativity with evidence-based active ageing practices to support physical, emotional, and social wellbeing.

"I've seen how movement transforms not only the body, but also a person's confidence and sense of purpose." "My goal is for everyone to leave every session feeling stronger, happier, and empowered to keep moving."

10+ Years in Dance Education & Community Arts
