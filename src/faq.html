01 / GETTING STARTED

A warm welcome starts here

What is Longevity Courtyard?

Longevity Courtyard supports active and healthy ageing through coach-guided exercise, holistic support, and community connections. Our programme focuses on strength, mobility, and independence in everyday life.

Do I need to have exercised before?

You do not need to be familiar with gym equipment to ask about getting started. Our sessions are coach-guided. Tell the team about your experience and goals so they can discuss a suitable starting point with you.

Can I try a session before choosing a plan?

Yes. We offer a one-time free trial session so you can experience a coach-guided session before choosing a plan. Contact the team to arrange your trial and confirm availability.

Book a free trial
How do I know which programme is right for me?

Start with a conversation with our team. Share what you hope to achieve and any support you may need. The team can explain the available options and what to expect before you book.

02 / PROGRAMMES & PLANS

Find your way to keep moving

What is the Shangri-La programme?

Shangri-La is a structured 12-week programme with coach-supervised group sessions, assessment support before and after the programme, and progress tracking. Training resistance is adjusted as you progress.

Explore our programmes
What plans are available?

You can start with a one-time free trial, choose the 3-month Shangri-La plan, or continue with a monthly Longevity Courtyard pass. Our Plans page has the prices and details to help you compare. Terms and conditions apply.

Compare plans
Will a coach guide me during sessions?

Yes. Longevity Courtyard sessions are supervised by coaches. You can get to know our fitness team before your visit.
